NARI 5.5.25

JJS enemy detection hardening.

- Increased JJS VLM output budget so target telemetry is less likely to be truncated.
- Reordered target fields to prioritize target visibility, target identity and target center.
- Added normalization for target_center and bbox-style outputs.
- Explicit enemy/opponent signals now activate human-target tracking even when target_visible was omitted.
- Human enemy fallback is restricted to explicit opponent signals to avoid confusing the local player with an enemy.
- Preserves coordinated advance_m1 and Dummy controls from previous releases.
