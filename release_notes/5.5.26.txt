NARI 5.5.26

JJS control pipeline fix.

- Decoupled VLM perception from the real-time input loop so slow vision inference cannot freeze controls.
- Added active recovery movement while the VLM is starting, unavailable, or stale.
- Increased camera reacquisition frequency when no target is visible.
- Added brief human-target persistence to survive individual missed VLM frames.
- Human targets now use coordinated advance_m1 once centered instead of relying on an unreliable VLM distance estimate.
- Compacted the JJS VLM response schema and reduced inference context/output for lower latency.
- Added live VLM age/target diagnostics to the game status.
