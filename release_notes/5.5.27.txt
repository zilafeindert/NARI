NARI 5.5.27

JJS target acquisition rewrite.

- Added a dedicated compact VLM target scanner instead of using the full gameplay JSON for enemy acquisition.
- The scanner explicitly distinguishes the local avatar, enemy avatars, and the green Dummy marker.
- JJS combat no longer depends on VLM-generated action lists for basic engagement.
- Centered human targets now use advance_m1 so movement and M1 happen together.
- VLM perception remains asynchronous, while local recovery keeps movement active during slow or failed vision.
- Added target persistence and faster camera reacquisition to avoid freezing after a missed frame.
