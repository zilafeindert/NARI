NARI 5.5.13

- Reworked JJS camera tracking into smooth bounded turn segments instead of frequent micro-corrections.
- Added eased mouse-delta interpolation so camera movement accelerates and decelerates naturally.
- Added a visual observation window after each camera correction before another correction is issued.
- Removed automatic per-frame camera direction inversion that could cause left-right oscillation.
- Kept target tracking limited to visible, confidence-backed targets.
