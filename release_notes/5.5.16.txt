NARI 5.5.16

Full audit follow-up.

Camera and targeting:
- JJS camera tracking is isolated from VLM inference and runs continuously from the local capture stream.
- Camera corrections use one serialized mouse owner, one-time cursor preparation and eased relative mouse motion.
- Removed competing VLM/local camera paths and stale camera state.
- Dummy detection is temporally associated and rejects weak/abrupt false positives.
- Human target centers are smoothed before use and remain synchronized with the camera thread.
- Target reacquisition is deliberately slow and does not switch away from a valid Dummy after one missed frame.

Combat:
- Added explicit JJS combat phases for approach, aim, engage, defend, escape, punish and guard reading.
- Confirmed-hit combo followups can override the base M1 attack when a valid skill candidate exists.
- Reward shaping strongly differentiates damage, hits, blocks, KO, movement/aim progress, whiffs, cooldown misuse, guard spam, damage taken, death and execution failures.
- Objective local Dummy distance and aim deltas are injected before reward calculation.
- Learned values influence action selection using contextual combat state.
- Negative rewards update the JJS policy faster than positive rewards.
- JJS action identifiers are profile-aware and legacy JJS identifiers are migrated without altering generic Roblox semantics.

Reliability:
- Fixed action-dispatch fallthrough in Computer.act.
- Game action validation is transactional so candidate evaluation does not consume cooldowns.
- Malformed VLM numeric/boolean fields are sanitized.
- Stopped/unsupported actions cannot be learned as successful executions.
- Game-loop exceptions release controls and surface an error instead of dying silently.
- Screen capture, voice listener, TTS, SQLite memory/decision stores and updater lifecycle are hardened.
- Updater ZIP extraction has path validation and rollback support.
- The updater creates its restart launcher dynamically and preserves data, voices, models, .venv and .env.
- Capture defaults are tuned for 30 FPS / 768px local tracking with 640px VLM analysis.
