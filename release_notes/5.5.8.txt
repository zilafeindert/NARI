NARI 5.5.8

- Reworked Jujutsu Shenanigans Dummy targeting to require stable consecutive detections.
- Added smoothing to the detected target center and small bounded camera corrections.
- Added adaptive camera direction correction when a turn moves the target away from center.
- Removed aggressive automatic camera sweeps when the Dummy is temporarily lost.
- Limited VLM-proposed JJS camera actions to small movements with a rate limit.
- Roblox camera input no longer recenters the cursor on every micro-turn and no longer sends an extra mouse pulse after releasing RMB.
- JJS approach/attack remains blocked until the Dummy is centered and visible.
